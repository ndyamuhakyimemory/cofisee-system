Our Story

COFISEE MICROFINANCE is a growing financial services business with a vision of promoting financial inclusion, supporting entrepreneurship and empowering individuals and small businesses through accessible financial solutions.

We believe that responsible access to finance can help people strengthen their businesses, manage opportunities and improve their livelihoods. Our approach is built around customer care, transparency and responsible lending.

As we grow, we aim to build a professional, trusted and technology-driven institution that contributes to employment creation and Uganda's economic development.

Our Financial Solutions

Our business focuses on customer-oriented credit and financial support. Services and products are provided only within the scope of the approvals and regulatory requirements applicable to the institution.

  • Loan facilities for eligible customers.
  • Clear repayment information and customer support.
  • Financial guidance to encourage responsible borrowing.
  • Business growth support for individuals and small enterprises.
